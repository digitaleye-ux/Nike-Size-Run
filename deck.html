04 · The problem

Late-loading photos, unreserved price boxes, and client-fetched inventory shove size buttons downward right as a shopper's thumb taps.

1. Pinching

Desktop layouts shrunk to a phone screen

Small text and tight buttons forced shoppers to pinch-to-zoom just to read US Men/Women size pairs.

2. Slow 4G

Three elements arrived late above the size grid

An unsized hero image, a price box with no min-height during font swap, and client-only fetch('/sizes.json') each changed page height after first paint.

3. Mis-taps

The grid jumped under the shopper's finger

When a shopper reached for M 8 as soon as it appeared, a late asset pushed the row down. Their thumb landed on the wrong button.

4. Returns

Wrong-size orders and wasted launch stock

Shoppers either hit a sold-out size and left, or ordered the wrong size and sent the shoes back.

Observed in Chrome DevTools under Slow 4G + 4× CPU throttling

05 · Key insight 1

Starting at 375 px first forces thumb-sized 44 px targets and ends pinch-to-zoom before a single line of desktop layout is written.

Phone screen order

1 Square 1:1 product photo (375 × 375 px)
2 Name, colorway swatch, and $140 price
3 2-row horizontal size rail (108 × 52 px buttons)
4 Full-width Add to Bag pill button

Why a 2-row horizontal rail won

I rejected a tall vertical grid of 26 buttons because it pushed Add to Bag below the fold. A 2-row horizontal rail keeps 6 to 8 sizes and the main CTA on the first screen, with every tap target at 108 × 52 px (well past the 44 px floor).

I also kept sold-out sizes visible in the rail rather than hiding them, so shoppers see what is gone instead of wondering why a size is missing.

Every button measures 108 × 52 px

06 · Key insight 2

Because a shopper's thumb moves the instant buttons appear, the size grid must stay frozen at 0 px shift from first paint.

Unlocked layout (what shifted)

Hero photo Collapsed until the image finished loading
Price box Changed height when the web font swapped in
Size data Waited on client-side fetch before drawing

Locked layout (0 px movement)

Hero photo Fixed 375 × 375 square reserved in CSS
Price box min-height: 32 px + self-hosted Inter woff2
Size data 26 sizes pre-hydrated on the server at t = 0
Alerts and low-stock messages render below the grid so they never push buttons

07 · Solution (page & stock)

Reserving every above-the-fold box and disabling the 9 sold-out sizes from the 8 December snapshot keeps taps accurate.

Studio WebP photos Two 750 × 750 images compressed to 23 KB and 28 KB
Locked price & font min-height: 28 px on price, font-synthesis: none, tabular-nums
Sold-out matching !in_stock || stock_status === 'sold_out' disables 9 sizes
Nike design system Applied DESIGN.md (#111111, #ffffff, #f5f5f5, pill CTAs)
Sold-out sizes stay visible, greyed out, struck through, and unclickable
