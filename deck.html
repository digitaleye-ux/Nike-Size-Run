04 · The Problem

Late-loading photos, unreserved price boxes, and client-fetched inventory shove size buttons downward right as a shopper’s thumb taps.

STEP 01 · VIEWPORT

Desktop layout shrunk to mobile

Unconstrained containers and small hit areas forced shoppers to pinch-to-zoom before they could read or tap sizes accurately.

STEP 02 · SLOW 4G ARRIVAL

Three elements jump above the grid

An unsized hero image, a price element without min-height during font swap, and client-side fetch('/sizes.json') all arrive late.

STEP 03 · THE MIS-TAP

Size grid shifts under the thumb

As the shopper commits to tapping M 8, late-arriving content pushes the grid down—landing their finger on the neighboring size instead.

STEP 04 · BUSINESS COST

Wrong-size orders & costly returns

Shoppers either hit a sold-out cell and bounce, or check out the wrong size—driving reverse-logistics cost and lost launch stock.

Why 2-Row Horizontal Scroll Beats Vertical Wrapping

Stacking 26 size buttons vertically pushes the Add to Bag button off the first screen. A compact 2-row horizontal rail keeps 6–8 sizes and the primary CTA visible above the fold—while holding every touch target at 108 × 52 px (above the 44 × 44 px WCAG floor).

07 · The Solution (Architecture)

Reserving every above-the-fold box and disabling the 9 sold-out sizes from the 8 December snapshot keeps taps accurate.

01 · Media Lock

Studio WebP + 1:1 Bounds

Replaced vector placeholders with two 750 × 750 WebP studio photos (23 KB & 28 KB) inside a fixed aspect-square stage with fetchPriority="high".

02 · Typography Lock

Price min-height + woff2

Locked [data-testid="price"] to min-height: 28px inside a 32px wrapper using self-hosted Inter with font-synthesis: none and tabular-nums.

03 · Stock Lock

Disabled Sold-Out States

Matched !in_stock || stock_status === 'sold_out' across all 26 sizes—rendering the 9 sold-out buttons greyed out, struck through, and disabled in place.

Styled with Nike DESIGN.md tokens (#111111 / #ffffff / #f5f5f5) Next: Tools, automated CI guardrails, and final results
